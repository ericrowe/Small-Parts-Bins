# Standalone 1x2 7U Gridfinity Cassette Bin (Plan 013)

This release implements a standalone **$1 \times 2$ Gridfinity-compatible cassette bin** at the standard **7U height** ($49.00\text{ mm}$ engaged shelf height, $53.40\text{ mm}$ total height with lip).
It sits directly on standard Gridfinity baseplates and features a continuous $1 \times 2$ Gridfinity stacking lip on top of its lid, allowing other Gridfinity bins to stack directly on top.

Two identical 7U bins stacked on top of each other measure **$102.40\text{ mm}$ total height**, fitting the measured **$111.125\text{ mm}$ inside drawer ceiling** with a generous **$+8.725\text{ mm}$ safety clearance margin**.

---

## Key Features

1. **Standard $1 \times 2$ Gridfinity Base:**
   - Two $42.0\text{ mm}$ standard Gridfinity base feet centered at $Y = \pm 21.0\text{ mm}$ with standard stepped $45^\circ$ lead-in profiles ($35.6 \to 37.2 \to 41.5\text{ mm}$).
   - Sits securely in any standard Gridfinity baseplate with zero wobble.
2. **7U Direct Stacking Lid:**
   - The lid top integrates a standard **$1 \times 2$ Gridfinity stacking rim** ($+4.40\text{ mm}$ height, $41.5 \times 83.5\text{ mm}$ outer, $37.2 \times 79.2\text{ mm}$ throat).
   - Standard Gridfinity bins (e.g. $1 \times 2$ or two $1 \times 1$ bins) stack directly onto the closed lid.
3. **Replaceable Glass Microscope Slide Window:**
   - End-loaded $27.0 \times 1.4\text{ mm}$ slide channel accommodating low-cost standard microscope slides ($75 \times 25 \times 1.1\text{--}1.2\text{ mm}$).
   - Centered $23.0 \times 55.0\text{ mm}$ clear viewing window.
   - Reinforced $1.20\text{ mm}$ solid PETG compliant retention clip (6 solid layers, 3D root gussets).
4. **Symmetrical Dual 9 mm Label Bands:**
   - Symmetrical $34.0 \times 10.0\text{ mm}$ flat solid label zones on both ends for standard 9 mm Brother TZe tape.
5. **Massive Hardware Capacity & Optional Dividers:**
   - Usable internal cavity: $37.5 \times 79.5 \times 43.0\text{ mm}$ deep.
   - Removable divider cards divide the cavity into 3 equal $25.0\text{ mm}$ compartments.
6. **Peaked 3-Knuckle Filament Hinge & Inward Clasp:**
   - Peaked support-free 3-knuckle hinge with $1.75\text{ mm}$ filament pin along the left long wall.
   - Reinforced $0.85\text{ mm}$ inward undercut clasp on the right long wall.
