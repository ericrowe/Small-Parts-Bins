## Outcome

Develop a standalone $1 \times 2$ Gridfinity-compatible cassette bin (7U height) that sits directly on standard Gridfinity baseplates and accepts standard Gridfinity bins stacked directly on top of its lid, while retaining the replaceable glass microscope slide window, positive slide capture, and internal dividers.

## Cancellation Rationale

During design iterations of both the embedded hinge mechanism and the subsequent horizontal sliding lid variant with stacking lip, the geometric complexity of integrating direct bin-on-bin stacking interfaces around a removable cassette lid proved excessive and problematic compared to the proven, physically verified **Cassette + Carrier Tray System Architecture** (Plans 001, 002, 003, 010, 011).

Per user directive on 2026-08-29 ("Cancel this experiment. We're not making progress."), this experiment was discontinued. The project maintains focus on the verified cassette family, removable dividers, dovetail pull tabs, 14U carrier trays, and fastener label generation.
