# Plan 013 Walkthrough — Develop Direct 1x2 7U Gridfinity Cassette Bin (CANCELED)

## Summary

Plan 013 explored developing a standalone $1 \times 2$ Gridfinity-compatible cassette bin (7U height) with a direct bin-on-bin stacking interface and replaceable glass microscope slide window.

## Iteration History

1. **Embedded Hinge Exploration:** Attempts to embed the peaked 3-knuckle filament hinge below the stacking lip encountered geometry conflicts between the hinge pin insertion bore and the divider channels.
2. **Horizontal Sliding Lid Variant:** A horizontal sliding lid architecture was developed with continuous guide rails recessed below the stacking lip and an open front entry mouth.
3. **Cancellation:** The design iterations highlighted excessive geometric complexity around the sliding guide tracks, corner closures, and finger pull mechanisms compared to the verified, highly functional **Cassette + Carrier Tray System Architecture** (Plans 001–004, 010, 011).

Per user directive on 2026-08-29, the experiment was canceled to preserve project focus on the proven modular cassette family and carrier ecosystem.
